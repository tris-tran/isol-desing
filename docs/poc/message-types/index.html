Message types

Polls, places, events and future secrets, each designed on its own in every state, incoming and outgoing, before they go into the chat.

Width
Chat
One widthEvery special message is 300px wide, or narrower on small screens. Nothing in them depends on the text around it.
Time in the footerThe time and ticks sit in a footer row (or the caption grid for places), never floating over content.
Light and darkIncoming bubbles are Parchment, outgoing are Aubergine. Lines, fills and soft text follow the bubble through shared variables.
Secrets keep their own lookSealed secrets are always the dark framed card, whoever sent them; unsealed, they turn light.
