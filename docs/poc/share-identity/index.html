Design notes

Why it looks like this

All light, on purpose

Share identity and Move identity are a pair. Here everything is public, so it sits on light plates with the green "Share" tag. Move identity puts its QR on the dark secret plate, behind a cover.

The Isol QR, small tier

A public key is tiny: 64 bytes on its own, 73 with the isol:pub: prefix. That puts it in the smallest tiers (dot 0.30 or 0.40), so Isolde shows clearly through the code.

Two forms

"Isol link" says what the code is, so the app can recognise it when scanned. "Key only" is the bare hex, for pasting into other tools. Copy always gives the exact text in the code.

A way across

A line under the key points to Move identity, so someone looking to use this identity on another device doesn't try to do it with the public key.
