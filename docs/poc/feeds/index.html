Ephemeral boards

Threads live while someone keeps them

Life you can see

Under every thread, a thin bar shows how many devices keep it. It shrinks from Olive to Gilt ("Fading") to Madder ("Last copy"), and the pictures lose their colour as it goes. When nobody keeps it, it leaves a dashed "faded away" trace for a moment, then it's gone.

Keep a thread

The seed button on a tile, or "Keep" in a thread, makes your device seed it. Kept threads glow Gilt and get their own "Kept" tab. Replying keeps a thread too, so conversations you're part of stay alive while you're around.

Sync a board

Syncing a board's catalog means new threads arrive as they're posted. A board you don't sync shows the snapshot from your last visit, with one tap to start syncing.

What it costs you

The status line says what this device does for the network: which boards it syncs, how many threads it keeps, and roughly how much space that takes.

Try it

"Time passes" plays the network: peers come and go, threads nobody keeps fade and disappear, and synced boards get new threads. "Skip a day" jumps twelve hours. Keep a fading thread and watch it survive.

Three ways to look

A · Catalog (dense tiles, best for scanning), B · Gallery (the pictures), C · Threads (the talk). The ephemeral layer works the same in all three.
