Isol POCs

One page per component, designed on its own before it goes into isol-svelte-components.

  • Create identityTwelve words, the private key and the public key. Real keys, made with isol's crypto.
  • Recover identityTwelve recovery words back into the same identity, checked word by word.
  • Share identityThe public key as an Isol QR code and as text. Nothing secret.
  • Move identitySend an identity to another device: a big secret QR code, or an encrypted file seeded as a torrent.
  • Log in with your phoneThe browser shows a temporary identity and magnet as an Isol QR; the phone scans it and approves. Both screens, step by step.
  • FeedsAn image board, but modern: three directions (catalog, gallery, threads) on the same boards, with a shared thread view.
  • IdentityEvery way an identity is shown (avatar, key, name, badges, mention, row, header, hover card, profile, compare), for every relationship.
  • Address bookContacts by public key: your name for them, their own name, and Verified / Trusted marks.
  • Message typesPolls, places, events and future secrets, each in every state, incoming and outgoing.
  • Network settingsWebRTC connections and swarms, the relay, trackers, and WebTorrent limits for everyday, priority and important files.
  • Mobile shellNavigation on a phone: Chats first, the bottom bar, long-press options, and one camera for every QR code.
  • ChatOne-to-one and group chats with photos and voice notes, plus the settings for each chat.
  • QR codesNine QR designs in Isolde's colours and shapes, each checked by a decoder, with a full-screen view for testing your scanner.
  • Halftone QRThe Isolde halftone at full print resolution, up to the largest QR code (version 40, 177×177).
  • isol-qr.jsThe halftone QR as one self-contained file. The demo needs a local server (ES module).
