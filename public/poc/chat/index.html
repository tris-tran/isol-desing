Design notes

Why it looks like this

Light by default

Parchment for what you receive, Aubergine for what you send, Vellum around it. No textures or wallpaper; colour is saved for meaning: Madder for actions and sender names, Gilt for read and verified, Olive for "on".

Bubbles that group

Messages from the same person run together: only the last one in a run gets the avatar and the tighter tail corner. Group messages show the sender's name in their own colour on the first bubble.

Photos fill the bubble

A single photo is 4:5. Several become an album in a 2×2 grid with a "+n" tile. With no caption, the time sits on the photo in a small pill.

Voice notes you can scrub

A play button, a waveform that fills as it plays, the time, and a speed chip. Click anywhere on the waveform to jump. Recording replaces the text field with a pulsing dot, a clock and a live waveform.

Peer to peer, honestly

A clock instead of ticks means the message is waiting for that person to come online. One tick is sent, two are delivered, Gilt is read.

Polls

Options are buttons; results, percentages and the first voters appear once you've voted, and you can change your vote. Single or multiple choice, with a closing time.

Location: a pin or live

A small map in the palette with a Madder pin, the place and its coordinates. Live location pulses, says how long is left, and stops by itself.

Events

A calendar-page date (Madder month, big day), the time and place, and Going / Maybe / Can't with counts and who's going. "Add to calendar" for your own calendar app.

Future secrets

A sealed card in Beardsley's triple-rule frame with Gilt hairlines. Shimmering bars stand in for the text, with the opening date, a live countdown, and the drand round it's locked to. When the time comes it glows: "Unseal" reveals the text.

The + menu

One button for everything you can send besides text and voice: photo, poll, location, event, future secret. Each opens a short sheet in place of the text field.

Names are yours

An identity is just a key, so the name you see is one you chose ("only you see this name"). The key is always one tap away, and you can verify it in person with the Isol QR.
