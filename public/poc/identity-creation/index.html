Design notes

Why it looks like this

Dark means secret

The words and the private key sit on Aubergine plates. The public key sits on Vellum. You can tell what is safe to share without reading a label.

The chain is the explanation

Plates go top to bottom in derivation order, with the step between each pair: words → private key → public key. The steps are named because this is the raw identity, not a profile.

Masked, not blurred

Hidden secrets are dashed bars of the same length for every word. Blur can leak the shape of the text and is harder to read as "hidden".

Hex in fours

The 64-character keys are split into 16 groups of four, with every other group slightly dimmer. That makes them easier to read aloud or compare. Copy always gives the plain hex.

One deliberate gate

"Use this identity" stays disabled until you confirm you saved the words. It is the only friction in the flow.

Real keys

The keys are made by isol's own crypto: BIP39 words, BIP32 at m/44'/1237'/0'/0/0 (NIP-06) and secp256k1 Schnorr x-only public keys. Nothing is stored or sent.
