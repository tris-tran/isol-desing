Design notes

Why it looks like this

Same plate as Create identity

The words are typed onto the same dark "Keep secret" plate they were shown on, in the same numbered grid. Recovering should feel like the mirror of creating.

Helps word by word

Each field checks itself against the 2048 recovery words: a green tick when it's one, a red mark when it isn't. While you type, matching words appear below; Tab or Enter takes the first.

Paste the whole phrase

Pasting twelve words into any field spreads them across the grid. Words can be hidden while typing in public.

Order matters, and it says so

Twelve real words can still be wrong together. The last word carries a checksum, so a swapped pair is caught: "these words don't fit together" points at order and spelling.

See who you'll be

Before confirming, the public key the words make is shown. If you know your key, you can tell it's the right identity before going on.

Honest about what comes back

Two short lists: what you get back (the identity, being recognised by people who know your key) and what you don't (messages, contacts, settings).
