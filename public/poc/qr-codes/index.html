How to read the results

"Decodes" is a floor, not a promise

The page renders each design to pixels and runs jsQR on it. A clean render is much easier than a phone camera, so a design that fails here will fail everywhere. One that passes still needs your scanner.

Scan view

Click a code (or "Scan view") to open it full screen. Arrow keys move between designs. Mark each one ✓ or ✗ as you go; marks are kept in this browser.

Contrast

The pill shows the worst contrast between any dark and light colour in the design. Above about 4:1 is comfortable. Below 3:1 depends on the scanner.

Risk labels

Safe: standard shapes and strong contrast. Bold: odd shapes, a logo or an image, so it needs H. Risky: inverted or low contrast; some scanners refuse these.

Error correction

Designs that cover modules (the jewel) or shrink them (halftone) lean on error correction. Keep H for those. Long payloads at H make denser codes: try the identity preset.
