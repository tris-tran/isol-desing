Design notes

How it hangs together

Chats is home

The app opens on Chats. The bar at the bottom holds the five places, Chats · Boards · Contacts · You · Settings, with an unread count on Chats and a Gilt dot when synced boards have new threads.

Tabs keep the bar, screens push over it

Inside a chat, contact info or a settings page, the bottom bar steps aside for the composer or the content. Back returns to the tab exactly where you left it.

You is a tab

Your own identity has a place in the bar, with your Vermilion avatar as the icon: your name, key and QR one tap away, ready to share or to compare.

Tap goes in, long-press asks

A contact opens straight into its chat. Holding it opens a sheet with the contact at the top and the things you'd do to them: message, info, verify, trust, rename, share, remove. Chats get their own sheet: pin, mute, mark read.

One camera

The camera button lives on Chats, the first screen. It reads any Isol QR and the sheet that follows says what it found: a contact to add, a browser to link, a group to join, or an identity to move here.

Same pieces as everywhere

Avatars, names, marks and rows come from the shared identity pieces, so the rules hold here too: quoted names, Gilt seals, and red only for you.
